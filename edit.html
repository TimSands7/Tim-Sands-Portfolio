    Publish your changes

    1. Click Download projects.js below.
    2. On GitHub, open your repo's js folder and click Add file → Upload files.
    3. Drag in the downloaded projects.js file (it replaces the old one) and click Commit changes.

    Your site updates within a couple of minutes. Your edits stay saved in this browser, so you can keep editing afterwards.
